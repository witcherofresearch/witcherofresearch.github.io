GitHub Profile

Currently, I am a research staff at TeleAI since June 2025. Previously, I was a research expert at Bytedance since Jan 2024 and a senior engineer at Alibaba. My research focuses on multi-reference-guided image/video generation since 2022, and video understanding during 2018 to 2022. I have practical industrial experiences dealing with catastrophic forgetting problems when introducing new controllable generation capabilities to the foundation models. I created and open-sourced TeleStyle V1 and V2 series for content-preserving style transfer given content reference and style reference, achieving comparable performance with closed-source competitors and were well-recieved by open-source community (more than 1k upvotes on Reddit) and industry (integrated by fal.ai). I was the owner of style reference customization functionality in 即梦2.0 era and launched the first style code function in China which was popular among users. I got my bachelor degree from Tsinghua University in 2018.

My current main skills are:

  1. Training experiences of multi-ref image customization on vlm+connector+dit and mixtures of transformers structures.
  2. Construction and synthesizing multi-ref training datasets.
  3. Joint training of multi tasks on image customization.
  4. Training with noisy data, curriculum learning, dealing with catastrophic forgetting.
  5. Training video generation model (minimax h3) to conduct mulit-ref image customization (content-preserving style transfer).
  6. Training SOTA reference to video (R2V) models.
